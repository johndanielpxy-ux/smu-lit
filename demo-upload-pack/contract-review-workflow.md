# AI-Assisted Sales Renewal Review

1. Capture the agreement, value, template version and counterparty.
2. Run extraction and semantic comparison in the firm-authorised legal AI.
3. Treat every AI finding and proposed route as unverified.
4. Open the submitted agreement and approved template for each material finding.
5. Open the supporting clause, enter the value you verified yourself, then apply the current playbook.
6. Escalate material deviations and uncertainty to legal review with a source-linked reason.

The responsible human remains accountable for the route decision.
